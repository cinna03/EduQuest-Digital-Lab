## Mission Statement

My mission at the African Leadership University is to design and build accessible digital learning experiences that help students in developing contexts learn through discovery, experimentation, and creation — not only through content delivery and memorization.

I am committed to using educational technology and digital innovation to:

- shift learning from passive consumption to active exploration;
- give learners safe spaces to experiment, fail, reflect, and try again;
- design tools that remain student-friendly, visually clear, and usable under real infrastructure constraints;
- create work that is locally relevant to Sub-Saharan African learning contexts while still meeting global standards of quality;
- build toward a future product vision — EduQuest — a student-centered ecosystem where classrooms are paired with digital laboratories and project studios so learners genuinely construct understanding.

This specialization project is the first concrete step toward that mission: a prototype digital laboratory experience in AR/VR that demonstrates how students can learn by experimenting in an interactive 3D space.

## Summary Note

**Project title:** EduQuest Digital Lab (Individual AR/VR Prototype)

**One-line objective:** Build a student-friendly digital laboratory in Unity where learners run short STEM experiments, observe cause-and-effect, and reflect on what they discovered — as the first module of a larger EduQuest learning ecosystem.

**Target users:** University / secondary students and educators in developing contexts (including ALU peers), plus course graders who need a desktop-playable demo without specialized hardware.

**Problem addressed:** Common LMS platforms (e.g. Canvas) are strong for course structure, content delivery, submissions, and grading, but weak for discovery, experimentation, and construction. EduQuest Discovery Lab prototypes the missing “lab room”: learning by doing, not only by downloading materials.

### Scope of the project

**In scope (this trimester):**
- One Discovery Lab product slice with **three stations**: Germination (science), Pendulum (physics), Dancing Blue Flame (chemistry simulation).
- Unity prototype with a desktop playtest scene for easy evaluation.
- Interactive variables per station with visible 3D response (sliders + ignite).
- Student-facing UI: menu, HUD, controls, status feedback, and a short reflection prompt (“What did you discover?”).
- Documentation path required by the specialization: ideation → Game Design Document → working prototype + walkthrough.

**Out of scope (this trimester):**
- Full LMS replacement (accounts, course libraries, gradebook, Canvas LTI).
- Full EduQuest Build Studio / multi-project workspace.
- Multiplayer, large content catalogs, or live online classes.
- Production-scale asset pipelines or multi-language CMS.

**Technology:** Unity (URP); desktop interaction for accessibility; AR Foundation / device placement as an optional enhancement. Design priority: clarity and experimental feedback over feature quantity.

**Success criteria:**
- A new user can understand and run one experiment in under two minutes.
- Changing a variable produces a clear, readable visual outcome.
- Reflection is prompted so learning is explicit.
- Graders can evaluate the experience on desktop without an AR headset/phone.
